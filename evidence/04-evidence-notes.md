\## 03 — Structured Alert CSV



Exported alert produced by the saved PowerShell detector during Simulation Mode.



\*\*Purpose:\*\* Demonstrate automatic generation of structured detection evidence.



\*\*Data classification:\*\* Simulated.



\## Evidence Integrity



Screenshots represent actual executions of the project. Any privacy redactions should obscure only sensitive identifying information, not change the results.



Simulation evidence must never be presented as evidence of an actual security incident.
